# ValleyShield AI

**Satellite Intelligence for Safer Communities**

ValleyShield AI is a geospatial disaster-intelligence platform that combines satellite-derived flood evidence, machine-learning-based flood segmentation, infrastructure exposure analysis, road-network connectivity modeling, and evidence-backed situation reporting.

## Problem

Floods and glacial outburst events can damage infrastructure and disrupt access to essential services. Satellite observations provide valuable evidence, but responders also need to understand which roads, buildings, and settlements may be affected.

## Proposed Solution

ValleyShield AI aims to:

- Detect potential flood extent from before-and-after satellite imagery.
- Evaluate a flood segmentation model on geographically unseen scenes.
- Identify potentially exposed roads, bridges, and buildings.
- Estimate settlement connectivity to selected towns and hospitals under documented road-disruption scenarios.
- Produce a map-based dashboard and downloadable situation report.

## Technology

Python, PyTorch, Rasterio, GeoPandas, OSMnx, NetworkX, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, and Leaflet.

## Scientific Integrity

The platform distinguishes satellite-observed flood evidence, model predictions, mapped infrastructure exposure, and inferred network accessibility. Satellite-based exposure is not treated as confirmed structural damage.
